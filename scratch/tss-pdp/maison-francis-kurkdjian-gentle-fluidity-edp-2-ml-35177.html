Francis Kurkdjian made his name at 25 with Le Male, and by the time he founded his own maison in 2009 he was among the most sought-after perfumers alive. Gentle Fluidity is one of his most conceptual releases — a pair of fragrances built from an identical list of ingredients, composed two different ways.

Launched in 2019.

Fragrance Notes

Top notes: Juniper Berries, Nutmeg
Heart notes: Coriander, Musk
Base notes: Amberwood, Vanilla

Fragrance Family

Woody Aromatic

The Experience

The Silver reading is the cooler, more aromatic of the two — juniper and musk over a soft woody base. Clean, bright and genuinely unisex.

Ideal Occasion

Everyday wear, office and cooler months.

Longevity

As an Eau de Parfum, expect 6–8 hours with moderate to strong projection on most skin types. Performance always varies with individual skin chemistry, humidity and the number of sprays applied.
